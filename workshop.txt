title=Animals Attack Zombies
description=Livestock that would stand up to a predator in real life now stand up to zombies. Every other animal still runs, as in vanilla. Each species can be turned off in the sandbox options. Install it on both the server and clients.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Bulls, boars and rams charge zombies that come near them or their herd.
description=[*]Roosters and turkey toms go after zombies that come near their flock. They are small, so they harass more than they hurt.
description=[*]A sow with piglets or a cow with a calf defends her young. Without them she runs like any other animal.
description=[/list]
description=
description=They behave like the real thing, and each of these can be changed or turned off in the sandbox options:
description=[list]
description=[*]They size a zombie up before committing. The closer it gets to them or what they guard, the likelier they are to charge. Mothers with young go fastest, and aggressive animals are quicker.
description=[*]They warn first: a bull stops, squares up and bellows before it comes, while a boar or a rooster barely pauses.
description=[*]They know when they are beaten. A crowd of zombies makes them run, though animals standing together hold out against more.
description=[*]Hurt animals do not fight.
description=[*]Fighting zombies doesn't stress them, so they don't break out of their pens or turn on players over it.
description=[*]They only go for a zombie they have a clear way to, not one on the far side of their fence.
description=[/list]
